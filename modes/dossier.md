# Mode: dossier — Deep research report (one prospect)

Deeper than grade-mode's Block D: **max 12 web searches**, stop at the cap,
mark gaps "unavailable". No research subagents. Every fact gets a source link.
Output: `data/dossiers/{NNN}-{company-slug}-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md` (or extend the
existing dossier with a `## Deep research` section).

## Six axes

1. **Strategy & priorities** — what they're building/selling, stated goals,
   positioning, pricing model; recent strategic shifts.
2. **Recent moves (≤6 months)** — funding, hires (esp. exec/eng), launches,
   partnerships, pivots, expansion. Date everything.
3. **Pain evidence** — hiring patterns (what roles, how long open), review-site
   complaints (supporting signal only), incident/status pages, public roadmap
   gaps, founder statements about challenges.
4. **Budget signals** — raise recency+size, headcount trajectory, pricing tier
   they sell at, visible tool spend (stack).
5. **Competitors & context** — who they compete with, their differentiation,
   category pressure (consolidation, new entrants, regulation).
6. **The user's angle** — given `profile/offer.yml` + `background.md`: the
   strongest fit line, which proof point leads, what to avoid, and the single
   best hook with its source.

## Output rules

Front-matter: company, contact(s), segment, date, search count used. Facts
labeled by axis with inline source links. End with a 5-line executive summary
and a recommended next action (grade / dm / email / nurture trigger).
